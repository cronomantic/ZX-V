10. Sound and music

In this chapter you will learn to make simple beeps with BEEP, to play tunes of up to six voices with PLAY, and to create sound effects for games with SOUND.

ZX-V has two kinds of sound: the beeper, a simple loudspeaker like the one in the first Spectrum, and two sound chips (called AY), like the one in the Spectrum 128, each able to play three notes at once.

BEEP#

BEEP duration,pitch plays one note. The duration is in seconds, and the pitch is the number of semitones above middle C (0 is middle C, 12 is the C an octave higher, -12 an octave lower):

10 BEEP 1,0
20 BEEP 0.5,12
30 BEEP 0.5,-12

The program waits while the note plays. With READ and DATA you can play a scale:

10 FOR i=1 TO 8
20 READ p
30 BEEP 0.25,p
40 NEXT i
50 DATA 0,2,4,5,7,9,11,12

PLAY: music in a string#

PLAY uses the sound chips and takes the music written in a string. The simplest strings are just note names:

10 PLAY "cdefgabC"

That is a scale: c is C (do), d is D (re), and so on; a capital letter is one octave higher. These are the most useful signs:

In the string Meaning
c d e f g a b Notes; in capitals, one octave higher
#c, $c Sharp and flat (the sign goes before the note)
& A rest (silence)
1 to 9 The length of the next notes: 3 a quaver, 5 a crotchet (the starting one), 7 a minim, 9 a semibreve
O n Octave, from 0 to 8 (it starts at 5, where c is middle C)
T n Tempo, in crotchets per minute (60 to 240; it starts at 120)
V n Volume, from 0 to 15

A length stays in force for the next notes until you change it. Spaces are ignored, so use them to make the tune easier to read:

10 LET m$="T100 O4 5ccgg aa7g 5ffee dd7c"
20 PLAY m$

Several voices#

Give PLAY several strings, separated by commas, and they sound at the same time: a tune and its accompaniment.

10 LET a$="T120 O5 3cegCegCe 7C"
20 LET b$="O3 7c&e&"
30 PLAY a$,b$

The first three strings use the three voices of the first chip; a fourth, fifth and sixth string use the second chip. Six voices at once:

10 PLAY "cdefg","efgab","gabCD","O3c","O3e","O3g"

The tempo is the one of the first string. PLAY waits until all the strings have finished; Esc stops it.

If you know the Spectrum: PLAY is the one from the Spectrum 128, with the same strings, extended to the second chip. The reference lists the rest of the signs: envelopes, repeats, triplets and more.

Sound effects with SOUND#

Games need sounds that are not notes: explosions, jumps, laser shots. SOUND talks to the sound chip directly. It writes a value into one of the chip's registers, its control knobs: SOUND register,value. You can write several at once, separated by ;. These are enough to start:

Register What it controls
0 and 1 The pitch of voice A: a period, so bigger numbers are lower notes. Register 0 takes 0 to 255, and register 1 adds steps of 256
6 The pitch of the noise (0 to 31)
7 The mixer: which voices play tone and which play noise. 62 means "voice A plays a tone"; 55 means "voice A plays noise"
8 The volume of voice A (0 to 15), or 16 to let the envelope control it
11 and 12 How long the envelope takes
13 The shape of the envelope: 0 fades out once

A zap that rises:

10 REM A rising zap on channel A
20 SOUND 7,62;8,15
30 FOR p=200 TO 20 STEP -6
40 SOUND 0,p;1,0
50 WAIT 1
60 NEXT p
70 SOUND 8,0

Line 20 turns on the tone of voice A at full volume, the loop lowers the period (so the note goes up) once every sixtieth of a second, and line 70 turns the volume down to 0. WAIT 1 waits for the next frame of the screen: you will see it again in the next chapters.

An explosion: noise that fades out by itself, thanks to the envelope:

10 REM An explosion: noise on channel A, with the envelope
20 SOUND 6,31;7,55;8,16;11,0;12,40;13,0
30 PAUSE 100

Unlike BEEP and PLAY, SOUND does not wait: the program goes on while the chip plays. That is what games need. The sound keeps playing until you change it, so remember to silence it with SOUND 8,0. Registers 16 to 31 are the same ones for the second chip.

Real music: MUSIC and SFX#

The music for a game is made with a tracker, a program for composing like the ones Spectrum musicians used. ZX-V comes with one for the PC, zxv-tracker, which saves the song in a .pt3 file and the sound effects in an .afb. BASIC plays them in the background, while the program goes on:

10 LOAD "tune" MUSIC: MUSIC PLAY
20 LOAD "effects" SFX
30 IF INKEY$=" " THEN SFX 0
40 GO TO 30

MUSIC STOP stops the music and SFX n plays effect number n without silencing it. Everything else is in the reference: MUSIC and SFX.

Try it#

  1. Play the beginning of a tune you know with PLAY.
  2. Make a "coin" sound for a game: two quick high notes, one after the other, with SOUND.
Hint

For the coin, turn on voice A (SOUND 7,62;8,15), set a period such as 60, wait a few frames with WAIT 4, set a smaller period (a higher note) such as 40, wait again, and turn the volume down with SOUND 8,0.

A solution for the second one 
10 SOUND 7,62;8,15
20 SOUND 0,60;1,0: WAIT 4
30 SOUND 0,40;1,0: WAIT 8
40 SOUND 8,0

Reference: BEEP, PLAY, SOUND, WAIT, MUSIC, SFX.
