5. Loops

In this chapter you will learn to repeat things with FOR ... NEXT, WHILE ... WEND and REPEAT ... UNTIL, to put one loop inside another, and to wait with PAUSE.

Counting with FOR ... NEXT#

A loop repeats a group of lines. FOR is the loop that counts:

10 FOR i=1 TO 5
20 PRINT "Lap ";i
30 NEXT i
40 PRINT "Finished!"

FOR i=1 TO 5 gives i the value 1 and runs the lines that follow. When it reaches NEXT i, it adds 1 to i and goes back, until i has gone past 5. So it prints Lap 1 to Lap 5, and then Finished!.

The variable that counts (here i) must have a one-letter name: FOR lap=1 TO 5 is a mistake.

STEP changes how much is added each time, and it can be negative or have decimals:

10 FOR i=10 TO 0 STEP -2
20 PRINT i;" ";
30 NEXT i
40 PRINT
50 FOR x=0 TO 1 STEP 0.25: PRINT x;" ";: NEXT x

This prints 10 8 6 4 2 0 and, on the next line, 0 0.25 0.5 0.75 1. A PRINT with nothing after it just ends the line.

Any of the numbers can be a variable or a sum. Here is a times table:

10 INPUT "Which times table? ";t
20 FOR i=1 TO 10
30 PRINT i;" x ";t;" = ";i*t
40 NEXT i

Loops inside loops#

You can put one loop inside another. Each one needs its own letter:

10 FOR r=1 TO 5
20 FOR c=1 TO r
30 PRINT "*";
40 NEXT c
50 PRINT
60 NEXT r

The outside loop counts the rows (r); for each row, the inside loop prints r stars. The result is a triangle:

*
**
***
****
*****

WHILE ... WEND: repeat while something is true#

Sometimes you do not know how many times to repeat. WHILE repeats while a condition is true, and checks it before each turn:

10 LET money=100: LET years=0
20 WHILE money<200
30 LET money=money*1.1: LET years=years+1
40 WEND
50 PRINT "Doubled after ";years;" years"

With 10 % interest every year, your money doubles in 8 years. If the condition is false from the start, the lines inside are never run.

REPEAT ... UNTIL: repeat until something happens#

REPEAT checks its condition after each turn, so the lines inside run at least once. It repeats until the condition becomes true:

10 RANDOMIZE
20 LET rolls=0
30 REPEAT
40 LET d=INT (RND*6)+1: LET rolls=rolls+1
50 PRINT d;" ";
60 UNTIL d=6
70 PRINT: PRINT "A six after ";rolls;" rolls"

With REPEAT, the guessing game from chapter 4 no longer needs GO TO:

10 RANDOMIZE
20 LET secret=INT (RND*100)+1: LET tries=0
30 REPEAT
40 INPUT "Guess my number (1-100): ";g
50 LET tries=tries+1
60 IF g<secret THEN PRINT g;" is too low"
70 IF g>secret THEN PRINT g;" is too high"
80 UNTIL g=secret
90 PRINT "Yes! ";tries;" tries"

Which loop should you use? FOR when you know how many times; WHILE when it may not need to run at all; REPEAT when it has to run at least once.

Waiting: PAUSE#

PAUSE n waits n fiftieths of a second, so PAUSE 50 is one second. PAUSE 0 waits until a key is pressed. A pressed key also cuts any pause short.

10 FOR n=10 TO 1 STEP -1
20 PRINT n
30 PAUSE 50
40 NEXT n
50 PRINT "Lift off!"

If you know the Spectrum: WHILE, WEND, REPEAT and UNTIL are ZX-V extensions; FOR, NEXT and PAUSE work as on the 48K.

Try it#

  1. Print the numbers from 1 to 20 with their squares (the number times itself).
  2. Write a program that asks for a word and prints it backwards: with HELLO it prints OLLEH.
Hint

For the second, w$(i) is letter number i. Go through the letters from LEN w$ down to 1 with STEP -1, and print each one followed by ;.

A solution for the second one 
10 INPUT "A word? ";w$
20 FOR i=LEN w$ TO 1 STEP -1
30 PRINT w$(i);
40 NEXT i
